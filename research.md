# Themes, training &amp; collaborators

<p class="lead">How simple local rules — a grain toppling, a fitness update, a noisy drive — produce scale-free behaviour, and what algebra and number theory can say about it.</p>
</div>

## Research themes

<div class="grid grid-2">
  <div class="card theme-card">
    <div class="formula">z<sub>c</sub> = 4 · ℤ<sup>n</sup>/Δℤ<sup>n</sup></div>
    <h3>Abelian sandpile models</h3>
    <p>Mathematical and computational study of sandpiles, including the directed Abelian sandpile on cylinders and its limits. Current postdoctoral work at GTIIT, at the crossroads with number theory.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card theme-card">
    <div class="formula">P(s) ∼ s<sup>−τ</sup> 𝒢(s/L<sup>D</sup>)</div>
    <h3>Self-organized criticality &amp; scaling</h3>
    <p>Avalanche dynamics, finite-size scaling and universality in critical avalanches; neuronal avalanches in the level model under noisy drive.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card theme-card">
    <div class="formula">max s ∼ L<sup>γ</sup></div>
    <h3>Extreme-event statistics</h3>
    <p>Scaling of extreme events in SOC models, including the 2d BTW sandpile.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card theme-card">
    <div class="formula">S(f) ∼ 1/f<sup>α</sup></div>
    <h3>Fluctuations &amp; nonlinear dynamics</h3>
    <p>Fitness fluctuations and correlation-time scaling in the barycentric Bak–Sneppen model; stochastic processes and renormalization-group ideas; chimera states (MSc).</p>
  </div>
</div>
